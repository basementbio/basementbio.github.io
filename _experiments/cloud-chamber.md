## Overview

Replace this text with a description of the experiment. A diffusion cloud
chamber makes ionizing radiation visible: a supersaturated layer of alcohol
vapor condenses along the trails left by charged particles.

## Method

1. Line the top of the tank with felt soaked in isopropyl alcohol.
2. Invert the tank onto a metal plate chilled with dry ice.
3. Darken the room and illuminate the chamber floor from the side.
4. Wait 10–15 minutes for the supersaturated layer to form, then observe tracks.
