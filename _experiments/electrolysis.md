## Overview

Replace with your write-up. This experiment demonstrates the 2:1 volume ratio
of hydrogen to oxygen predicted by the chemical formula of water.

## Method

1. Dissolve a spoon of baking soda in water to improve conductivity.
2. Submerge two graphite electrodes, each capped with an inverted test tube.
3. Apply a DC voltage and record gas volumes over time.

## Results

Note the measured H₂:O₂ ratio and how close it came to the theoretical 2:1.
